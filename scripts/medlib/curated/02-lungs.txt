= asthma | Asthma | lungs | ph
mp: Asthma
aka: hika, hirap huminga, wheezing, asma
wiki: Asthma
s: Asthma makes the breathing tubes swell and narrow.
s: It comes in attacks of wheeze, cough and tight chest.
s: A reliever inhaler and a plan from your doctor keep it safe.
g: Lips or face turn blue or grey
g: Cannot speak a full sentence, or struggles for each breath
g: The reliever inhaler does not help, or helps for less than a few hours
g: Call 911 or go to the hospital now
what: Asthma is a long-term illness of the breathing tubes. They become swollen and tight, and make extra mucus. Dust, smoke, cold air, colds, pets and strong smells can start an attack. It can be well controlled.
signs: Wheezing, a whistle when breathing out ; Cough, worse at night or early morning ; Tight chest ; Short of breath
home: Sit upright, stay calm and use your reliever inhaler as your doctor taught you ; Take your daily controller medicine even when well ; Stay away from smoke, dust and mold ; Keep a written asthma plan
prev: Learn and avoid your triggers ; Do not smoke and avoid smoky rooms ; Wash bedding often ; Get a flu shot every year

= copd | COPD (long-term lung disease) | lungs | ph
mp: COPD
aka: emphysema, chronic bronchitis, sakit sa baga, hirap sa paghinga, ubong matagal
wiki: Chronic_obstructive_pulmonary_disease
s: COPD slowly makes it harder to breathe out.
s: Smoking is the main cause.
s: Stopping smoking and using inhalers slows it down.
g: Breathing is much harder than usual, or you cannot speak in full sentences
g: Blue lips or fingers, or new confusion or drowsiness
g: Chest pain, or coughing up blood
what: COPD is a long-term lung illness that includes chronic bronchitis and emphysema. The air tubes are damaged and narrowed, so air gets out slowly. It does not go away, but treatment and no smoking help you feel better and slow it down.
signs: Cough that lasts for months, often with phlegm ; Short of breath with walking or stairs ; Wheezing ; Tight chest ; Many chest infections
home: Stop smoking and stay away from smoke and cooking smoke ; Use inhalers as prescribed ; Get flu and pneumonia shots ; Ask about breathing exercises and rehab
prev: Do not start smoking, or quit ; Use a chimney or open a window when cooking with wood ; Wear a mask in dust ; Treat chest infections early

= pneumonia | Pneumonia | lungs | ph
mp: Pneumonia
aka: pulmonya, impeksyon sa baga, lung infection, namamaga ang baga
wiki: Pneumonia
s: Pneumonia is an infection that fills the lungs with fluid.
s: It can be mild, or it can be life-threatening.
s: Babies, older people and sick people need care fast.
g: Fast, hard or painful breathing, or chest pulling in with each breath
g: Blue lips, or a child too sleepy to feed or drink
g: Confusion in an older person, or a high fever with shaking
g: Coughing up blood
what: Pneumonia is an infection of the lungs caused by germs such as bacteria and viruses. The tiny air sacs fill with fluid and it becomes hard to breathe. It can come after a cold or flu. Antibiotics help only when the cause is bacteria, so a doctor must decide.
signs: Cough with phlegm ; Fever and chills ; Fast breathing and short of breath ; Chest pain when breathing or coughing ; Feeling very tired
home: See a doctor, do not treat it alone ; Take the full course of any medicine prescribed ; Rest and drink plenty of fluids ; Do not smoke

mp: Tuberculosis
aka: TB, tuberkulosis, ubong may dugo, sakit sa baga, pagdura ng dugo
wiki: Tuberculosis
s: TB is a germ that spreads in the air and mostly harms the lungs.
s: Free tests and treatment are given at health centres.
s: It can usually be cured if you take all the medicine, usually for six months or more.
g: Coughing up blood
g: Chest pain, or very hard breathing
g: High fever with severe weakness, or a child who is very sick
what: TB is an infection caused by a germ. It spreads when a person with TB in the lungs coughs or sneezes. Not everyone who is infected gets sick. TB is curable with medicines taken every day for months. Stopping early can make it come back stronger.
signs: A cough that lasts more than two weeks ; Coughing up blood ; Night sweats and fever ; Weight loss and no appetite ; Tiredness
home: Take all medicine every day for the whole time, even when you feel well ; Cover your mouth when you cough and open windows ; Go to the health centre for free TB tests and care (DOTS) ; Eat well
prev: Test people living with someone who has TB ; Open windows to let in fresh air ; Complete the full treatment so it cannot spread ; Have newborns vaccinated with BCG

wiki: Sleep_apnea
s: Breathing stops for a few seconds again and again while you sleep.

s: It raises the risk of high blood pressure and stroke.

mp: Whooping Cough
aka: pertussis, whooping cough, ubo ng sanggol, ubong tuloy-tuloy
wiki: Whooping_cough
s: Whooping cough is a serious cough illness, most dangerous for babies.
s: A vaccine in the baby shots protects against it.
s: A baby with long coughing fits needs a doctor at once.
g: A baby who stops breathing, turns blue, or gasps after coughing
g: Vomiting after coughing, in a baby or child

what: Whooping cough is a bacterial infection of the airways. It causes long coughing fits that end with a "whoop" sound when the child breathes in. Babies may not whoop, but may stop breathing. Antibiotics help if given early.

home: See a doctor, mostly for babies ; Keep the child away from others until treated ; Small feeds more often ; Keep the child upright after a fit
