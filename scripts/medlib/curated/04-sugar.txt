mp: Diabetes Type 2
aka: diabetes, type 2 diabetes, asukal, mataas na asukal, sakit sa asukal, blood sugar
wiki: Type_2_diabetes
s: Your body cannot use sugar well, so it builds up in the blood.
s: Over the years high sugar harms eyes, kidneys, nerves and feet.
s: Food, activity, medicine and regular checks keep it in range.
g: Very sleepy or confused, breathing fast, or fruity-smelling breath
g: Vomiting that does not stop, or cannot keep fluids down
g: Sugar very high with extreme thirst and passing a lot of urine
g: A foot sore with fever or black skin
g: Shaking, sweating, confusion or passing out (low sugar): give sugar if awake and able to swallow, call 911 if not
what: In type 2 diabetes the body does not make enough insulin or does not use it well. Sugar then stays high in the blood. It is often linked to weight, family history and age. It can be controlled and sometimes put into remission, but it needs long-term care.
signs: Very thirsty ; Passing urine a lot, especially at night ; Tired, blurred vision ; Slow-healing sores ; Tingling in the feet; many people have no signs
home: Take medicines or insulin as prescribed ; Eat regular meals with less sugar and white rice, more vegetables ; Walk after meals if your doctor allows ; Check your feet daily ; Keep check-ups, eye and kidney tests
prev: Keep a healthy weight ; Move 30 minutes on most days ; Cut sweet drinks ; Get your sugar tested from age 35, or earlier if family has diabetes

= diabetes1 | Type 1 diabetes | sugar
mp: Diabetes Type 1
aka: type 1 diabetes, juvenile diabetes, diabetes sa bata, insulin
wiki: Type_1_diabetes
s: In type 1 diabetes the body makes no insulin.
s: It often starts in children and young people.
s: Insulin every day is a must, and never stopped.
g: Vomiting, tummy pain, fast breathing, fruity breath (a sign called DKA)
g: Very sleepy, confused or unconscious
g: Sugar very high and ketones in the urine
what: Type 1 diabetes happens when the body's own defence harms the cells that make insulin. Insulin must be given by injection or pump for life. With good care, children and adults can live full lives. Missing insulin can cause a dangerous emergency called diabetic ketoacidosis.
signs: Very thirsty and hungry ; Passing a lot of urine ; Weight loss ; Tired ; Stomach pain or fruity breath if serious
home: Take insulin exactly as taught ; Check sugar as advised ; Always carry quick sugar for lows ; Never skip insulin when sick ; Keep clinic visits
prev: It cannot be prevented ; Early diagnosis prevents emergencies ; Teach family and school what to do for lows

mp: Prediabetes
aka: prediabetes, borderline na asukal, mataas na asukal sa dugo, pre-diabetes
wiki: Prediabetes
s: Prediabetes means your sugar is above normal but not yet diabetes.
s: Many people do not know they have it.
s: Activity and weight loss can turn it back.
g: Extreme thirst, passing lots of urine, blurred vision: see a doctor soon
g: Very sleepy or confused: go to the hospital
what: In prediabetes, blood sugar is higher than normal but not high enough for diabetes. Without change, many people get type 2 diabetes within a few years. Losing even a small amount of weight and walking daily can cut the risk a lot.
signs: Usually none ; Darker skin on the neck or armpits ; Found on a blood test
home: Walk 30 minutes most days ; Lose a little weight: about 4 to 5 kg if you weigh 70 kg ; Eat less sugar and white rice, more vegetables ; Re-test your sugar every year
prev: Keep a healthy weight ; Move every day ; Cut sweet drinks ; Do not smoke

= hypoglycemia | Low blood sugar | sugar
mp: Hypoglycemia
aka: hypoglycemia, low sugar, mababang asukal, nanginginig, hilo sa gutom
wiki: Hypoglycemia
s: Sugar that is too low can make you shaky, sweaty and confused.
s: It is most common in people who use insulin or some diabetes pills.
s: Quick sugar fixes it in minutes.
g: The person cannot swallow, is very confused, or has a fit
g: The person passes out: call 911, do not put anything in the mouth
g: Sugar does not rise after 15 minutes of treatment, or they pass out
what: Low blood sugar, usually under 70 mg/dL in a person with diabetes, can come on quickly. Missing a meal, extra exercise, too much insulin or alcohol are common causes. Treat it at once. Unchecked it can cause fits and unconsciousness.
signs: Shaky, sweaty, fast heartbeat ; Hunger, headache ; Confusion, odd behaviour ; Blurry vision ; Fainting
home: If awake: give 15 g of quick sugar, such as half a glass of juice or 3 teaspoons of sugar in water ; Re-check in 15 minutes and repeat if still low ; Then eat a snack or meal ; Tell your doctor it happened
prev: Eat meals on time ; Always carry sugar or candy ; Check sugar before driving and exercise ; Talk to your doctor about medicine doses

= diabeticfoot | Diabetic foot care | sugar | ph
mp: Diabetic Foot
aka: diabetic foot, sugat sa paa, paa ng diabetic, ulcer sa paa, sugat na hindi gumagaling
wiki: Diabetic_foot
s: High sugar can numb the feet and slow healing.
s: A small sore can turn into a serious infection.
s: Check your feet every day.
g: A sore with pus, redness, warmth or a bad smell
g: Black or blue skin on a toe or foot
g: Fever with a foot sore, or a hot, swollen foot
what: Diabetes can damage nerves and blood vessels in the feet. You may not feel a cut or blister, and it heals slowly. Untreated sores can lead to infection and, in the worst cases, loss of a toe or foot. Daily care prevents most of this.
signs: Numbness or tingling ; Burning pain ; Dry, cracked skin ; Cuts or blisters you did not feel ; Colour change
home: Look at your feet and between toes every day ; Wash with lukewarm water, dry well ; Moisturise but not between toes ; Wear clean cotton socks and shoes that fit ; Never walk barefoot ; Do not cut corns yourself
prev: Keep sugar in range ; Do not smoke ; Get a foot check at every visit ; Cut toenails straight across

= diabeticeye | Diabetes and the eyes | sugar
mp: Diabetic Eye Problems
aka: diabetic retinopathy, malabo ang mata, eye problems in diabetes, mata at diabetes
wiki: Diabetic_retinopathy
s: High sugar can damage the tiny vessels in the eye.
s: It can cause blindness, but early signs are silent.
s: A yearly eye check protects your sight.
g: Sudden loss of vision, or a curtain over the vision
g: Many new floaters or flashes of light
g: Sudden eye pain or redness with blurred vision
what: Diabetic retinopathy is damage to the back of the eye. At first there may be no symptoms. Without treatment it can lead to blindness. Laser or injections can protect sight if found early. Good sugar and blood pressure control slows it.
signs: Often none at first ; Blurred or wavy vision ; Dark spots (floaters) ; Poor night vision ; Colours look dull
home: Get an eye exam with dilated pupils every year ; Keep sugar and blood pressure in range ; Do not smoke ; Tell your eye doctor you have diabetes
prev: Control sugar, blood pressure and cholesterol ; Yearly eye checks ; Stop smoking

mp: Thyroid Diseases
aka: thyroid, bosyo, goiter, hyperthyroid, hypothyroid, hipo, pamamaga sa leeg
wiki: Thyroid
s: The thyroid is a small gland in the neck that controls energy.
s: It can work too fast or too slow.
s: A blood test finds it and medicine treats it.
g: Trouble breathing or swallowing with a neck swelling
g: Very fast heartbeat, high fever and confusion
g: Extreme sleepiness or very cold body and confusion
what: The thyroid makes hormones that control how your body uses energy. Underactive thyroid makes you tired, cold and heavy. Overactive thyroid makes you hot, shaky and thin. A swelling in the neck (goitre) can have many causes. Treatment is often a daily tablet or other care.
signs: Under: tiredness, weight gain, constipation, feeling cold ; Over: weight loss, racing heart, sweating, anxiety ; A lump or swelling in the neck
home: Take thyroid medicine at the same time each day on an empty stomach ; Do not stop without your doctor ; Keep blood test follow-ups
prev: Use iodised salt ; See a doctor for a lump in the neck ; Do not smoke

mp: Diabetes and Pregnancy
aka: gestational diabetes, diabetes sa buntis, buntis na may diabetes
wiki: Gestational_diabetes
s: Some women get high sugar for the first time in pregnancy.
s: It usually goes away after birth, but raises the risk of later diabetes.
s: Good control protects mother and baby.
g: Baby moves much less than usual

g: Headache, vision changes or swelling of the face
what: Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar that starts in pregnancy. It can make the baby grow large and cause problems at birth. A sugar test between weeks 24 and 28 checks for it. Food, activity and sometimes insulin keep it under control.
signs: Often none ; Very thirsty ; Passing more urine ; Tiredness
