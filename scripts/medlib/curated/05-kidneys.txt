= ckd | Chronic kidney disease | kidneys | ph
mp: Kidney Diseases
aka: CKD, kidney disease, sakit sa bato, bato, kidney failure, dialysis
wiki: Chronic_kidney_disease
s: Your kidneys slowly lose the power to clean the blood.
s: Diabetes and high blood pressure are the top causes.
s: Early care can slow it down, but it cannot be felt early.
g: Very little or no urine, or swelling of the face and legs that gets worse
g: Trouble breathing, or chest pain
g: Confusion, vomiting that will not stop, or fits
what: The kidneys clean waste and extra water from the blood. In chronic kidney disease they slowly lose this power over months or years. There is often no sign until late. Controlling blood pressure and sugar, a kidney-friendly diet and avoiding certain painkillers protect what is left.
signs: Often none early ; Swollen feet or puffy eyes ; Tired, poor appetite ; Foamy urine ; Itchy skin
home: Take blood pressure and sugar medicines as told ; Avoid ibuprofen and similar painkillers and herbal remedies unless your doctor agrees ; Eat less salt ; Ask for a diet plan, with limits on some foods ; Get blood and urine tests regularly
prev: Control blood pressure and diabetes ; Do not smoke ; Drink enough water ; Get yearly urine and blood tests if you are at risk

= kidneyfailure | Kidney failure and dialysis | kidneys | ph
mp: Kidney Failure
aka: kidney failure, dialysis, hemodialysis, dialysis, PhilHealth dialysis, bato bumigay
wiki: Kidney_failure
s: When the kidneys fail, a machine or a transplant must do their work.
s: Dialysis cleans the blood several times a week.
s: PhilHealth helps cover dialysis sessions.
g: Missed dialysis and now short of breath, swollen or very tired
g: Chest pain, very fast or slow heartbeat, or muscle weakness
g: Signs of infection around the dialysis catheter: fever, redness, pus
what: In kidney failure the kidneys work at less than 15 of every 100 as normal. Waste and water build up. Treatment is dialysis (a machine cleans the blood) or a kidney transplant. Dialysis works only if you attend every session and follow the diet and fluid limits.
signs: Very tired and weak ; Nausea, poor appetite ; Swelling ; Short of breath ; Itchy skin
home: Never miss a dialysis session ; Follow limits on water, salt, potassium and phosphate ; Keep the access site clean ; Take medicines as told
prev: Control diabetes and blood pressure ; Avoid unneeded painkillers ; Treat kidney disease early

= kidneystone | Kidney stones | kidneys | ph
mp: Kidney Stones
aka: kidney stones, bato sa bato, nephrolithiasis, masakit ang tagiliran
wiki: Kidney_stone_disease
s: Stones are hard lumps that form in the kidney from salts.
s: They cause sudden, very bad pain in the side.
s: Drinking lots of water helps pass them and prevents more.
g: Fever or chills with side pain
g: Cannot pass urine, or blood in urine with pain
g: Pain so bad you cannot sit still, or constant vomiting
what: Kidney stones form when minerals in the urine clump together. Small stones can pass on their own with fluids and pain relief. Bigger stones may need a procedure. Dehydration is a common cause, so heat and sweating matter.
signs: Sharp pain in the side or back, can move to the groin ; Blood in the urine ; Burning or urgent urination ; Nausea or vomiting
home: Drink plenty of water, enough to pass pale urine ; Take pain relief as the doctor advises ; Strain your urine if told, and keep the stone for testing ; Follow-up with the doctor
prev: Drink 2 to 3 litres of water daily ; Eat less salt ; Eat more fruit and vegetables ; Limit animal protein

= fattyliver | Fatty liver | kidneys | ph
mp: Liver Diseases
aka: fatty liver, taba sa atay, fatty liver disease, atay, liver
wiki: Fatty_liver_disease
s: Extra fat stored in the liver is very common.
s: Most people feel nothing.
s: Weight loss and less sugar can reverse it.
g: Yellow skin or eyes with tummy swelling
g: Vomiting blood or black stools
g: Confusion or very sleepy
what: Fatty liver means too much fat builds up in the liver. It is linked to being overweight, diabetes, high cholesterol, and sometimes alcohol. In time it can cause inflammation and scarring. It is often found on an ultrasound. Lifestyle changes work best.

aka: cirrhosis, scarred liver, pamamaga ng tiyan, atay na may peklat
wiki: Cirrhosis
s: Cirrhosis is deep scarring of the liver.
s: Long-term alcohol and hepatitis B or C are common causes.
s: Stopping the cause protects what is left.
g: Vomiting blood or black stools
g: Confusion, sleepiness or odd behaviour

signs: Tiredness and weakness ; Yellow skin and eyes ; Swollen tummy and legs ; Easy bruising ; Itchy skin
