= hypertension | High blood pressure | heart | ph
mp: High Blood Pressure
aka: hypertension, altapresyon, mataas na presyon, mataas ang dugo, BP
wiki: Hypertension
s: The force of blood on your blood vessel walls stays too high.
s: Most people feel nothing, so only a check can tell.
s: Over the years it can harm the heart, brain and kidneys.
g: Sudden weakness or numbness of the face, arm or leg, or trouble speaking
g: Crushing chest pain or pain that spreads to the arm or jaw
g: A very bad headache with confusion, or trouble seeing
g: A reading of 180/120 or higher with chest pain, short breath, a bad headache, or trouble seeing or speaking: call 911
what: Blood pressure is the push of blood against the walls of your blood vessels. When the top number stays at 140 or higher, or the bottom number at 90 or higher, it is called high blood pressure. Your doctor will say what number is right for you.
signs: Often none at all ; Headache, dizziness or a pounding in the chest can happen, but not always ; A check at the health centre or at home is the only sure way
home: Take your medicine every day, even when you feel well ; Eat less salt, patis, toyo and instant noodles ; Walk most days if your doctor says it is safe ; Do not smoke, and limit alcohol
prev: Check your pressure at least once a year, more often if you are over 40 ; Keep a healthy weight ; Eat more vegetables and fruit ; Move your body every day

= heartattack | Heart attack | heart | ph
mp: Heart Attack
aka: myocardial infarction, atake sa puso, inatake, sakit sa puso
wiki: Myocardial_infarction
s: A heart attack happens when blood cannot reach part of the heart.
s: It is an emergency, and every minute counts.
s: Fast treatment can save the heart and save a life.
g: Chest pain or pressure that lasts more than a few minutes
g: Pain in the arm, back, neck or jaw, with sweating or feeling sick
g: Trouble breathing, or feeling faint with chest discomfort
g: Call 911 or go to the nearest hospital now. Do not drive yourself.
what: A heart attack happens when a blocked blood vessel stops blood from reaching part of the heart. The heart muscle starts to be damaged. Women, older people and people with diabetes may have fewer clear signs.
signs: Pressure, squeezing or heavy feeling in the chest ; Pain that moves to the arm, back, neck or jaw ; Cold sweat, feeling sick, short of breath ; In women and older people: unusual tiredness or tummy discomfort
home: While you wait for help, sit down and stay calm ; Loosen tight clothes ; If a doctor has told you to take aspirin for this, follow that advice ; Never wait to see if it passes ; If the person collapses and is not breathing normally, start hands-only CPR
prev: Keep blood pressure, sugar and cholesterol in check ; Do not smoke ; Eat less salty and fatty food ; Stay active and keep a healthy weight

= stroke | Stroke | heart | ph
mp: Stroke
aka: brain attack, na-stroke, pagkaparalisa
wiki: Stroke
s: A stroke is a sudden loss of blood flow to part of the brain.
s: Learn BE FAST: Balance, Eyes, Face, Arm, Speech, Time.
s: Getting to a hospital in the first hours can save a life.
g: One side of the face droops when the person smiles
g: One arm is weak or numb, or cannot be lifted
g: Speech is slurred or strange, or the person cannot understand you
g: Sudden loss of balance or vision, or a very bad headache. Call 911 now and note the time it began.
g: Go now even if the signs go away after a few minutes.
what: A stroke happens when a blood vessel in the brain is blocked or bursts. Brain cells start to die within minutes. Quick treatment can save brain and life. High blood pressure is the biggest cause.
signs: Face drooping on one side ; Arm or leg weakness on one side ; Slurred speech or confusion ; Sudden trouble seeing or walking ; Sudden very bad headache
home: Do not give food, drink or medicine by mouth ; Lay the person down with the head a little raised ; Note the time the signs began and tell the hospital ; After a stroke, follow your rehab plan and take medicines as told ; Do not prick the fingers or ears
prev: Keep blood pressure under control ; Do not smoke ; Stay active ; Eat less salt and less fatty food ; Take heart medicines as prescribed

mp: Angina
aka: angina pectoris, pananakit ng dibdib, sikip ng dibdib
wiki: Angina
s: Angina is chest pain when the heart does not get enough blood.
s: It often comes with effort and eases with rest.
s: It is a warning sign. See a doctor soon.
g: Chest pain that lasts more than 5 minutes, or does not ease with rest or your heart medicine: call 911 now. Do not drive yourself.
g: Pain with sweating, feeling faint, or trouble breathing
g: Pain that is new, worse, or comes at rest

signs: Pressure or tightness in the chest ; Pain that spreads to the shoulder, arm, neck or jaw ; Short of breath or tired with effort ; Pain that eases after rest

= heartfailure | Heart failure | heart
mp: Heart Failure
aka: weak heart, mahinang puso, pagpalya ng puso, namamaga ang paa
wiki: Heart_failure
s: The heart is weak and cannot pump blood as well as it should.
s: It does not mean the heart has stopped.
s: Medicines, less salt and daily checks keep people well.
g: Sudden bad shortness of breath, or you cannot lie flat
g: Chest pain, fainting, or a very fast heartbeat
g: Coughing up pink, foamy spit
what: In heart failure the heart muscle is too weak or stiff to pump enough blood for the body. Fluid can then build up in the lungs, legs and tummy. It is a long-term illness that can be managed well.
signs: Short of breath, especially lying down ; Swollen feet, ankles or tummy ; Tiredness and weakness ; Quick weight gain over a few days ; Cough at night
home: Take medicines every day ; Weigh yourself each morning and tell your doctor about a quick gain ; Eat less salt ; Ask your doctor how much to drink ; Keep your follow-up visits
prev: Keep blood pressure and sugar under control ; Do not smoke ; Treat heart disease early ; Limit alcohol

= cholesterol | High cholesterol | heart | ph
mp: Cholesterol
aka: cholesterol, mataas na kolesterol, taba sa dugo, dyslipidemia
wiki: Hypercholesterolemia
s: Too much cholesterol in the blood can block blood vessels over time.
s: You cannot feel it. A blood test shows it.
s: Food, activity and sometimes medicine bring it down.
g: Chest pain or pressure, or signs of a stroke, are an emergency. Call 911.
what: Cholesterol is a fatty substance in your blood. Some is needed, but too much LDL ("bad") cholesterol can build up in your blood vessels. This raises the chance of heart attack and stroke. Family history can also play a part.
signs: Usually none ; Found by a blood test called a lipid profile

mp: Arrhythmia
aka: arrhythmia, atrial fibrillation, AFib, palpitations, pagtibok ng puso, mabilis na tibok ng puso
wiki: Atrial_fibrillation
s: The heart beats too fast, too slow, or in an uneven way.
s: Many cases are harmless, but some raise the risk of stroke.
s: A doctor can check it with a heart tracing (ECG).
g: Fainting, or chest pain with a fast heartbeat
g: A fast heartbeat with trouble breathing
g: Signs of a stroke: face droop, weak arm, slurred speech

= cpr | Cardiac arrest and CPR | heart
mp: CPR
aka: CPR, cardiopulmonary resuscitation, heart stopped, hindi humihinga, nawalan ng malay
wiki: Cardiopulmonary_resuscitation
s: In cardiac arrest the heart stops and the person collapses.
s: Hands-only CPR can keep blood moving until help arrives.
s: Anyone can do it. You cannot make it worse.
g: A person who suddenly collapses, will not wake and is not breathing normally: call 911 now
what: When the heart stops, blood stops reaching the brain. Hands-only CPR means pushing hard and fast on the centre of the chest, about twice a second. Do this until help or an AED arrives, or the person wakes.
signs: Collapse without warning ; Will not wake when you shout and shake the shoulders ; Not breathing, or only gasping
home: Call 911 or ask someone to call ; Send someone to find an AED (a shock machine) if there is one ; Place the heel of one hand on the centre of the chest, the other hand on top ; Push down hard, about 5 cm for an adult, and let the chest come back up ; Push fast, about twice a second ; Keep going without stopping until help takes over
prev: Learn CPR from the Red Cross ; Treat heart disease early ; Know where the nearest AED is in your building

wiki: Deep_vein_thrombosis
s: A blood clot can form in a deep vein, often in the leg.
s: The clot can travel to the lungs and be life-threatening.
s: Long sitting, bed rest and surgery raise the risk.
g: Sudden short breath or chest pain, especially with a swollen leg
