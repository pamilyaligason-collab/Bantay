Get the Bantay app

For our family's Android phones. About 2 minutes.

Opened this from Messenger? Tap the three dots and choose Open in Chrome first. The download only works in Chrome.
  1. 1
    DownloadChrome may warn about the file. Tap Download anyway, then Open.
  2. 2
    Allow Chrome to install appsAndroid asks once: tap Settings, turn on "Allow from this source", then go Back.
  3. 3
    InstallIf Play Protect asks to scan the app, let it scan, then tap Install.
  4. 4
    Open BantayJack Jack meets you there.
Didn't see it after the download? Open Files, then Downloads, and tap Bantay.
If you have the old Bantay icon (web version), long-press it and choose Uninstall once the app works.

Bantay on iPhone stays a web app

Nothing to download. About 2 minutes.

  1. 1
    Open this link in SafariOpened it from Messenger? Tap the Safari icon or Open in browser first.
  2. 2
    Tap ShareThe square with an arrow, at the bottom of Safari.
  3. 3
    Tap Add to Home Screen, then AddBantay now opens from its own icon, like an app.
There is no APK on iPhone, so there is no file to download or install. Allow notifications when Bantay asks.
