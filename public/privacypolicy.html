Family Bantay privacy

Draft, not yet approved. The family admin reviews this page before the word Draft is removed. Last updated 2026-11-06.

Family Bantay is a private app for one family. This page says what it keeps, who can see it, where it goes and how you control it.

What it keeps

  • Your name, your sign-in and your choices in the app.
  • Health records you or your family add: blood pressure, sugar, weight, medicines, doses taken, papers and photos, mood check-ins.
  • Steps, sleep, heart rate and blood pressure from your phone, only the kinds you turn on. Bantay only reads them; it never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health.
  • When the Family map is on in the app, the exact latest place and battery level of people who sign in is shared with their family by default, updated when Bantay opens and, in the Android app from version 2.8.0, once a day in the background (that daily run shares only the battery level and that the phone checked in, never a new place), and off any time in Me, Privacy. Turning sharing off, or signing out, stops the background update and ends its key. Only the latest point is kept, deleted when turned off or after 7 days without a new place.
  • The Family feed is off by default. For each person, and each kind (blood pressure, lab results, photos, check-ins), the person switches that kind on for themself, and for a family member without a login the family owner may switch it on. Only the kinds switched on are shown to the family in the feed, and each can be switched off any time in Me, Privacy. The family place is separate and is not part of the feed.
  • Messages to your family, SOS records and alerts.
  • When you agreed to this notice.

Who can see it

Only you, the family owner, and the people you or the family owner share with can see your records. Nothing is shared by default, except your latest place and battery on the Family map (listed above). In Me, Privacy, Who can see my records, you pick a family member and they can see your records at once. They are told, and you can stop it at any time. The list is kept on the server, so it is the same on every phone. The family owner can see everyone's records, to help. Only the owner and you can set who sees your records, and you will be told. For a family member who has no sign-in, the owner and their carers set it. A log records who changed what. Nobody outside your family circle can see your records. They are not sold and not used for ads.

Where it goes

Your numbers are saved on your phone first, then sent to these services so the family owner and the people you or the family owner share with can see them. The app sends data only to the services below. Other official sites open in your browser only when you tap their link.

  • Supabase: the family database, with your records and messages. It also holds the map files the Safety maps and the Rescue map read from the family's own project, and sees your internet address and which map file was read. When a hazard level is read online, it also sees which part of the file, which shows your area to within about 10 km.
  • Open-Meteo and Windy: weather forecast and live map. They receive your place rounded to about 1 km, never your name. With Labs, Weather blend on, Bantay's own server also asks MET Norway and, when the family has set up a key, OpenWeather for the same rounded place; they see the server's internet address, not yours.
  • ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com): the US dollar rate on Today, once a day. It receives no name, place or health data; it sees your internet address.
  • Wikipedia: the "On this day" facts in Discover. It receives today's date only, never your name or place.
  • Wikimedia Commons and news sites: recipe photos load from Wikimedia Commons, and a news story's picture loads from the site that published it, when its card shows. Each sees your internet address, never your name.
  • YouTube: picture previews load when a video card shows, so YouTube sees your internet address. The video loads only when you tap.
  • Love Radio, Easy Rock, DZRH, Home Radio and Magic 89.9: only when you tap Play on a radio card. The station sees your internet address. Love Radio, Easy Rock and DZRH stream from their own servers, Home Radio from radioca.st and Magic 89.9 from Triton (streamtheworld.com).
  • OpenStreetMap: street map pictures on the Safety maps, the Rescue map and the Family map. Offline maps are on by default: the Safety maps use the area map for Bohol and South Cotabato once it is on the phone, and these pictures are used when a Safety map opens outside Bohol and South Cotabato, opens zoomed out, or before the area map is on the phone; the choice is made when the map opens, so after panning away they stay as they started. With Labs, Offline maps off, every map uses them. It sees your internet address and the area shown. Barangay names on Today, Safety and Weather come from OpenStreetMap place names (© OpenStreetMap contributors) kept in small files on the phone: the app downloads the file for your one-degree square (about 110 km wide) from its own host, Cloudflare Pages, which sees your internet address and that square (near its edge, also the square next to it), then reads it offline.
  • Ventusky, HazardHunterPH and PAGASA Panahon: the map panels in Safety, only after you open a panel. Ventusky gets the map centre rounded to about 1 km. The other two get no place from the app. Each sees your internet address and anything you type in its panel. PAGASA Panahon also loads when you tap Load the map on the Today map card; it gets no place from the app.
  • MedlinePlus: the plain health reads in Discover and Health. It receives the condition, medicine or search words you open, never your name.
  • OpenRouter: the AI models behind Jack and photo reading, only when you use them, and only the text or photo you share. What you ask Jack is sent through a relay on the family owner's own server first; a photo you scan goes from a function on the family's own Supabase project straight to OpenRouter, with no relay. Zero data retention is switched on. The servers are outside the Philippines. When you turn AI off in Me, Privacy, Jack and photo reading stop sending anything, and papers you scan from then on are kept but not read by the AI. Papers you scanned earlier that were not read yet may still be.
  • Google Drive: copies of papers, in the folder the family owns.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): only in the Android app, when you allow notifications. It carries a short alert in set words, with no names or readings, and Google sees your phone's push address.
  • Cloudflare Pages: hosts the app.

Alerts

On the Android app, family alerts show inside Bantay when you open it, and medicine alarms ring on your own phone. In a browser that allows it, an alert can also arrive as a notification. A notification never shows a reading or a result.

Your choices

  • Turn a switch off in Me, or remove access in Health Connect or your phone Settings. New reads stop. Records already saved stay until you delete them.
  • Download my data in Me, Privacy: one file with your records and readings.
  • Delete my data in Me, Privacy. Deletion runs after 7 days and you can undo it until then. It removes your readings, medicines, papers, messages and alerts. A log of who changed what is kept. Copies in the family's Google Drive folder are removed by the folder owner.

These match your rights under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 to be informed, to see your data, to correct it and to have it deleted.

Agreement

When you join, you agree that the family owner can see your health records and that you can choose to share them with others. The family owner can always see your records. Anyone else needs your OK or the owner's. You are told, and you can stop them. Bantay keeps the date. Me, Privacy shows it. People who joined before this was recorded get today's date the first time they open Me or Privacy.
