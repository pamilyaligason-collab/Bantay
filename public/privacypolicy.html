Family Bantay privacy

Draft, not yet approved. Last updated 2026-10-17.

Family Bantay is a private app for one family. This page says how it uses health data from your phone.

What it reads

Only the kinds of data you turn on in the app: steps, sleep, heart rate, blood pressure. It asks your phone for each one separately. It only reads. It never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health.

Where it goes

The numbers are saved on your phone first, then shared with your family circle in Family Bantay so they show on the Health screens. The shared copy is stored in the family's Supabase database. The numbers are not sold, not used for ads, and not shared with anyone outside the family circle.

Notifications

Health numbers never appear in a notification.

Turning it off

Turn a switch off in the app, or remove access in Health Connect or your phone Settings. Turning a switch off stops new reads. Data already saved stays until the family admin removes the synced copies from the database. The copy on your phone stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Family Bantay is not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat anything. Talk to your doctor about your health.
