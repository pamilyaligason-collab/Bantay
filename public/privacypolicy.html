Family Bantay privacy

Draft, not yet approved. The family admin reviews this page before the word Draft is removed. Last updated 2026-10-27.

Family Bantay is a private app for one family. This page says what it keeps, who can see it, where it goes and how you control it.

What it keeps

  • Your name, your sign-in and your choices in the app.
  • Health records you or your family add: blood pressure, sugar, weight, medicines, doses taken, papers and photos, mood check-ins.
  • Steps, sleep, heart rate and blood pressure from your phone, only the kinds you turn on. Bantay only reads them; it never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health.
  • Your place, only when you turn on live location. Weather uses your place rounded to about 1 km.
  • Messages to your family, SOS records and alerts.
  • When you agreed to share your health records with your family.

Who can see it

Only you, the family admins, and the people you share with can see your records. Nothing is shared by default. In Me, Privacy, Who can see my records, you pick a family member and send them an offer; they see nothing until they accept, and you can stop at any time. The list is kept on the server, so it is the same on every phone. The family admins can see records to help. The family admins can also set who sees your records; you will be told, and you can stop it. A log records who changed what. Nobody outside your family circle can see your records. They are not sold and not used for ads.

Where it goes

Your numbers are saved on your phone first, then sent to these services so the family can see them. No other service is used in this version.

  • Supabase: the family database, with your records and messages.
  • Open-Meteo and RainViewer: weather and rain maps. They receive your place rounded to about 1 km, never your name.
  • Wikipedia: the "On this day" facts in Discover. It receives today's date only, never your name or place.
  • OpenRouter: the AI models behind Jack and photo reading, only when you use them, and only the text or photo you share. Zero data retention is switched on. The servers are outside the Philippines. When you turn AI off in Me, Privacy, Jack and photo reading stop sending anything, and papers you scan from then on are kept but not read by the AI. Papers you scanned earlier that were not read yet may still be.
  • Google Drive: copies of papers, in the folder the family owns.
  • Cloudflare Pages: hosts the app.

Alerts

On the Android app, family alerts show inside Bantay when you open it, and medicine alarms ring on your own phone. In a browser that allows it, an alert can also arrive as a notification. A notification never shows a reading or a result.

Your choices

  • Turn a switch off in Me, or remove access in Health Connect or your phone Settings. New reads stop. Records already saved stay until you delete them.
  • Download my data in Me, Privacy: one file with your records and readings.
  • Delete my data in Me, Privacy. Deletion runs after 7 days and you can undo it until then. It removes your readings, medicines, papers, messages and alerts. A log of who changed what is kept. Copies in the family's Google Drive folder are removed by the folder owner.

These match your rights under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 to be informed, to see your data, to correct it and to have it deleted.

Agreement

When you join, you agree to share your health records with your family. Bantay keeps the date. Me, Privacy shows it. People who joined before this was recorded get today's date the first time they open Me or Privacy.

If something goes wrong

See the breach plan.

Family Bantay is not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat anything. Talk to your doctor about your health.
