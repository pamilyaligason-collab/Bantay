Typhoon "Samuel"

Issued at 11:00 am, 09 November 2026
(Valid for broadcast until the next advisory to be issued at 5:00 PM today)

TYPHOON CEMPAKA HAS ENTERED THE PHILIPPINE AREA OF RESPONSIBILITY AND IS GIVEN THE LOCAL NAME “SAMUEL”
  • HAZARDS AFFECTING LAND AREAS
  • Heavy Rainfall Outlook
  •  
  • SAMUEL is less likely to directly affect the weather condition of the country throughout the forecast period.
  • Severe Winds
  •  
  • The Northeast Monsoon will bring strong to gale-force gusts over the following areas (especially in coastal and upland areas exposed to winds):
  • Today: Most of Luzon and Visayas, Dinagat Islands, Surigao del Norte, Misamis Oriental, Camiguin, Lanao del Norte, Misamis Occidental, Zamboanga del Norte, Davao Oriental, Davao Occidental, and Sarangani.
  • Tomorrow (10 November) until Wednesday (11 November): Most of Luzon and Visayas, Dinagat Islands, Surigao del Norte, Misamis Oriental, Camiguin, Misamis Occidental, Zamboanga del Norte, Davao Oriental, Davao Occidental, and Sarangani.
  • HAZARDS AFFECTING COASTAL WATERS
  • A Gale Warning may be issued this afternoon over the northern seaboard of Northern Luzon due to the Northeast Monsoon.
  • 24-Hour Sea Condition Outlook
  • Up to very rough seas over the following coastal waters:
  • Up to 5.5 m: The northern seaboard of Batanes.
  • Up to 5.0 m: The northern seaboard of Babuyan Islands; the remaining seaboard of Batanes.
  • Up to 4.5 m: The northern seaboard of Ilocos Norte; the northeastern seaboard of mainland Cagayan; the remaining seaboard of Babuyan Islands.
  • Sea travel is risky for all types or tonnage of vessels. All mariners must remain in port or, if underway, seek shelter or safe harbor as soon as possible until winds and waves subside.
  • Up to rough seas over the following coastal waters:
  • Up to 4.0 m: The seaboards of Isabela and northern Aurora; the northern seaboard of mainland Cagayan; the northern and eastern seaboards of Polillo Islands; the remaining seaboard of Ilocos Norte.
  • Up to 3.5 m: The seaboards of Ilocos Sur, Isabela, and northern Quezon; the northwestern seaboard of Pangasinan; the northern seaboards of Camarines Norte and Kalayaan Islands; the remaining seaboard of Aurora.
  • Up to 3.0 m: The seaboard of northern La Union; the northern seaboard of Camarines Norte; the northern and eastern seaboards of Catanduanes; the northeastern seaboards of Northern Samar and Eastern Samar.
  • Mariners of small seacrafts, including all types of motorbancas, are advised not to venture out to sea under these conditions, especially if inexperienced of operating ill-equipped vessels.
  • Up to moderate seas over the following coastal waters:
  • Up to 2.5 m: The southern seaboard of Catanduanes; the eastern seaboards of Albay and Sorsogon; the northern seaboard of Northern Samar; the eastern seaboards of Eastern Samar, Dinagat Islands, and Siargao-Bucas Grande Islands; the remaining seaboards of La Union, Pangasinan, and Kalayaan Islands.
  • Up to 2.0 m: The seaboards of Zambales, Lubang Islands, Palawan including Calamian, Cuyo, and Cagayancillo Islands, Caluya Islands, Zamboanga del Norte, and Surigao del Sur; the eastern seaboard of Davao Oriental; the southern seaboards of Oriental Mindoro and Occidental Mindoro; the remaining northern and eastern seaboards of Quezon; the remaining seaboards of Polillo Islands, Camarines Norte, and Catanduanes.
  • Mariners of motorbancas and similarly sized vessels are advised to take precautionary measures while venturing out to sea and, if possible, avoid navigation under these conditions.
  • Other coastal waters have forecast wave heights of less than 2.0 m.
  • TRACK AND INTENSITY OUTLOOK
  • It must be emphasized that heavy rainfall and severe winds may still be experienced in localities outside the landfall point and the forecast confidence cone. Refer to “Other Hazards affecting Land Areas” for more details. Furthermore, the track may still shift within the limit of the forecast confidence cone.
  • SAMUEL is forecast to turn north northeastward slowly within the next 12 hours and exit the Philippine Area of Responsibility (PAR) between tonight and tomorrow (10 November) early morning. Once outside the PAR, SAMUEL will accelerate generally east northeastward over the Pacific Ocean.
  • SAMUEL is forecast to continue intensifying and may reach its peak intensity by this evening or tomorrow early morning. Afterwards, it will then enter a weakening trend due to unfavorable conditions.
Location of Eye/center

The center of Typhoon SAMUEL was estimated based on all available data at 1,370 km East Northeast of Extreme Northern Luzon (23.9 °N, 134.7 °E )

Movement

Moving North Northwestward at 20 km/h

Strength

Maximum sustained winds of 120 km/h near the center and gustiness of up to 150 km/h
