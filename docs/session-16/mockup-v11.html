    High blood pressure

    Part 2 of 6 · about 3 minutes

    What it is

    Blood pushes against the walls of your blood vessels. When it pushes too hard for too long, the heart and kidneys work harder than they should.

    How you can feel

    Most people feel nothing. That is why we measure it, even on good days.

    What helps
    • Take your medicine at the same time every day.
    • Use less salt, patis and bagoong.
    • Walk a little each day, if your doctor agrees.
    Blood pushing on the vessel wallsOnline
    Get help now if
    • Very bad headache with blood pressure above 180/120
    • Chest pain, trouble speaking, or one side weak
    Source: MedlinePlus, National Library of Medicine, High Blood Pressure topic (public domain). Not a replacement for Dr. Santos.

    Mama's medicine

    Losartan 50 mg

    For blood pressure · 1 tablet at 9:00 AM after breakfast

    What it does

    Losartan relaxes your blood vessels so blood flows with less push. It also protects your kidneys.

    Tight, then relaxed
    How to take it
    • One tablet, same time each day, with or without food.
    • Keep taking it even when you feel well.
    If you miss a doseTake it when you remember, unless it is almost time for the next one. Never take two at once.
    Common side effects
    • Dizzy when you stand up fast. Stand slowly.
    • Tired or a stuffy nose in the first weeks.
    Call 911 nowFace, lips or tongue swelling, or trouble breathing: call 911.
    Do not mix with
    • Potassium supplements
    • Pain pills like ibuprofen or naproxen (NSAIDs)
    • Salt substitutes made with potassium ("lite salt")
    Food notesUse herbs, calamansi and vinegar instead of salt. Check the label: "low sodium" salts often contain potassium.
    Talk to your doctor firstAsk Dr. Santos or your pharmacist before you stop, skip or change this medicine.
    Where it comes fromGeneric name: losartan potassium. Brand names you may see: Cozaar, Lifezar. Many companies make the generic. Your pharmacist can tell you which one you have.

    Malunggay (moringa)

    Leaves, powder or capsule

    How good is the evidence?
    LittleGood = many strong studies. Some = a few studies. Little = small or early studies.
    What people use it for

    As a food, malunggay leaves have vitamins and minerals. Studies on blood sugar and blood pressure are small and early, so it does not replace your medicine.

    Possible interactions
    • May lower blood sugar more with Metformin
    • May lower blood pressure more with Amlodipine or Losartan
