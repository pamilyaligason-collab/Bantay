# Phone check (about 20 minutes, a real phone)

Do this on your own phone with the new app installed and signed in. Tick each step. If a step does not give the expected result, write down the step number and what you saw.

**Before you start:** tell your family you are testing SOS. A real hold sends a real alert (call and text) to your SOS contacts.

## 1. SOS hold (S: J2)
1. Tap the Jack button. The Jack sheet opens. Expect: a red "Hold for help" button and three "Try saying" chips.
2. Press and hold "Hold for help". Expect: a ring fills over 3 seconds, the words count down, and "Nothing is sent yet" shows.
3. Let go after 1 second. Expect: it cancels, nothing is sent.
4. Hold the full 3 seconds. Expect: the SOS screen opens and your family is alerted. Only do this after you have told your family (see Before you start).
5. Press the Android Back button on the SOS screen. Expect: the SOS stays active or asks first. It must not silently drop an alert in progress. Write down what you saw.
6. During a real weather warning (if one is up): do steps 1 to 3 again. Expect: the same behaviour, and the Hold button is not covered.

## 2. Status bar and bottom edge (S1, S2)
1. Open Today, then Health, then Safety. Expect: no text sits under the clock or battery at the top.
2. Look at the bottom. Expect: the tab bar floats clear of the phone's gesture line, with no strip of the wrong colour below it.
3. Turn the phone sideways and back. Expect: the same on both.

## 3. Steps
1. Open the app and wait for the first sync (about 1 minute).
2. Open Health, then Steps. Pick one full day that is over.
3. Compare with Huawei Health for the same day. Expect: within about 1%, up to the last finished hour.

## 4. Sleep and sensor switches
1. Open Health, then Sleep. Expect: last night shows hours and times that match Huawei Health.
2. Open Settings, Phone. Expect: every sensor switch you had on is still on.

## 5. Sending works
1. Log one blood pressure reading.
2. On a carer's phone, open Bantay. Expect: the reading shows there within a few minutes.

Done. Send back the list of any steps that did not match.
