Five steps

1Check
Is it safe? Tap the shoulders and shout. If there is no answer and no normal breathing (or only gasping), start.
2Call 911
Call 911, or ask someone to call and bring an AED if there is one. Put the phone on speaker.
3Hands on the centre of the chest
Lay the person on their back on a firm, flat place. Heel of one hand on the centre of the chest, other hand on top, arms straight.
4Push 5 to 6 cm
Push straight down about 5 to 6 cm (2 inches), then let the chest come fully back up. Two pushes every second or so.
5Keep going until help arrives
Do not stop until help takes over, an AED is ready, or the person moves or breathes normally. Swap with someone every 2 minutes if you can.
Not trained? Hands-only CPR is OK.
Doing something is better than waiting. This is for adults. Children and babies need different steps.
