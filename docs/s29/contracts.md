Hourly chance = share of blend sources with precipitation >= 0.1 mm for that hour when the blend has >= 2 sources, else the model probability. UI: "Raining now" chip with "observed at <station>" or "model"; a rain chance under 10% reads "Low chance", never "0%". No location fix -> show "Location off: showing <place>" instead of silently using Tagbilaran.
